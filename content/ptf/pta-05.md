# PTF tips: PTA-05 Find air conduction thresholds

- Count the ascending runs, not all the responses. A response on the way down does not count towards the 2 of 3.
- If responses come at a steady rhythm, the patient is following you, not the tone. Change your timing.
- Glance at the retest at 1 kHz. If it differs by more than 5 dB, stop and re-instruct before you go further.
