- Before you start bone conduction, predict the answer from your history and tympanometry. A surprise result is a cue to check placement.
- Vibrotactile responses hide in the low frequencies of severe losses. Ask "hear or feel?" every time you pass 50 dB HL at 500 Hz and below.
